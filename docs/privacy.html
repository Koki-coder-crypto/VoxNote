VoxNote Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

VoxNote records audio and processes speech recognition and memo highlights on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your recordings, transcripts, notes, or purchase history. We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking, or sell personal information.

Microphone and speech-recognition permissions are used only for the recording and transcription features you start. Your recordings and notes remain under your control in the app.

Apple processes App Store purchases and subscriptions under its own privacy policy. VoxNote receives only the entitlement information necessary to unlock Pro features.

For support or privacy questions, visit VoxNote Support.
