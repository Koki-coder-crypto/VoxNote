## Store copy

### English

**Subtitle:** Record, transcribe, and act.

**Description:** VoxNote keeps short meetings, interviews, and personal memos organized from recording to follow-up. Start a recording, review the on-device transcript, then edit key points and action items before sharing. Audio and transcript text stay on your device. No account, advertising SDK, or cloud upload is required.

**Keywords:** voice memo,transcription,recorder,meeting notes,dictation,offline,speech

## Review notes

No login is required. Tap Record, allow microphone and speech recognition, record a short memo, stop, and open the memo to review or edit the transcript. VoxNote requires Apple on-device speech recognition and does not upload audio or transcript text. The annual subscription has a seven-day trial; the monthly plan has no trial. Restore Purchases is available on the paywall.
