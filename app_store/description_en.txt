VoxNote turns a recording into a private, structured memo.

Whether it is a meeting, lecture, idea, or reminder, VoxNote records, transcribes, and creates a memo overview on your device.

WHAT VOXNOTE DOES

RECORD
Tap once to record. Pause, resume, or cancel at any time. No limits on the number of recordings.

TRANSCRIBE
Powered by Apple's on-device speech recognition engine, VoxNote converts your audio to accurate text - no audio ever leaves your device.

MEMO OVERVIEW
After a recording, VoxNote organizes a short overview, key points, and next actions for your review.

ORGANIZE
Browse all your memos with search. Every memo includes the original audio so you can replay it anytime.

EXPORT
Share transcripts and summaries via the system share sheet - copy to Notes, send via Messages, or export as text.

PRIVACY

- Speech recognition runs entirely on your device - no audio is sent to external servers
- Your recordings are stored only on your device
